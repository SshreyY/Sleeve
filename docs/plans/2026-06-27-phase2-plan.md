## Step 2.2: Paycheck History & Analytics
- **Purpose:** Let users review past paychecks and track their savings rate over time.
- **Backend Tasks:**
  - Create endpoint `GET /api/paychecks/history` that returns a historical list of paychecks with total savings rates.
- **Frontend Tasks:**
  - Create a "History" modal or a collapsable panel inside the Chat Dashboard.
  - Show a small line-chart tracing the **Total Savings Rate** over time, allowing the user to see how their savings rate fluctuates.

### How to Verify
1. Enter multiple paychecks via chat (e.g., $4,000, $4,500, $5,000).
2. Ask Sleeve: *"Show me my income history"* or click the dashboard history toggle.
3. Verify that the graph renders the historical trend accurately.
